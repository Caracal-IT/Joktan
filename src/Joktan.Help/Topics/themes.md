# Themes

A theme holds all the colours, fonts and text sizes of the app. Each has a page under Settings.

## Choosing a theme

On **Settings / Themes**, choose one of:

- **Follow system**: Light or Dark, according to your operating system. This is how Joktan starts.
- **Light** and **Dark**.
- **Green Dark**: dark, with green surfaces, menu and accent.
- Any theme you saved.

The choice applies at once and is remembered.

## Making your own

1. Choose the theme closest to what you want.
2. Change what you like on **Colours**, **Fonts** and **Text Sizes**. Each change shows at once.
3. In the **Theme** card at the top of the page, type a name and press **Save as new**.

To change a theme you saved, change its values and press **Save**. The themes that come with Joktan
stay as they are, so their changes can only be saved as a new theme. **Discard** goes back to the theme
as it was last saved.

Changes not saved are lost when Joktan closes. Another theme cannot be chosen until they are saved or
discarded.

To rename a theme, save it as new under the other name and delete the old one.

## Colours

Each colour has a picker, and a box to type it in, such as `#3BB001`. A box with a red outline does not
hold a colour yet, and has changed nothing.

**Dark controls** gives text boxes, switches and other controls their look for a dark background. Turn it
on when a theme's background is dark.

## Fonts

Each list holds the fonts on this computer. **Headings**, **Brand** and **Menu** follow the **Interface**
font until you give them their own. Use a monospaced font for **Code**, so that message bodies and
columns line up.

A theme can name a font another computer does not have. It then uses Inter there.

## Text sizes

Sizes go from 8 to 72, with a sample beside each. Icons, spacing and the height of rows stay the same, so
very large text can be cut off.

## Deleting a theme

**Delete** on the Themes page removes a theme you saved, straight away. If it is in use, Joktan goes back
to following the system.
